# 📱 11Mercado PTA Manual SMS Registration
## Simple Opt-In System for Grade 11 Parents

**Easy, personal, and direct SMS notification system!**

---

## 👥 **For Parents: How to Get SMS Notifications**

### **Step 1: Contact the Section President**
- **WhatsApp, text, or talk in person** to the Grade 11 Mercado Section President
- **Say**: "I want SMS notifications for my child's attendance"
- **Provide**: Your phone number and your child's full name

### **Step 2: Confirmation**
- Section President will add your number to the system
- You'll get a test SMS to confirm it works
- **That's it!** No complex registration needed

---

## 📨 **What SMS You'll Receive**

### **When Your Child is Present:**
> *11Mercado PTA Alert: [Your Child's Name] marked PRESENT today (Sep 7, 2025 at 7:30 AM). Safe learning environment confirmed. ✅*

### **When Your Child is Absent:**
> *11Mercado PTA Alert: [Your Child's Name] marked ABSENT today (Sep 7, 2025 at 8:00 AM). Reason: Sick with fever. Please ensure student safety. ⚠️*

## ❓ **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: Is this really free?**
**A:** Yes! The section president has Globe unlimited SMS, so no cost to anyone.

### **Q: Will you share my number?**
**A:** No. Your number is only used for attendance alerts and stored securely.

### **Q: Can I get notifications for multiple children?**
**A:** Yes! Just mention all your children when registering.

### **Q: What if I change my number?**
**A:** Just inform the section president of your new number.

### **Q: Can other parents see my information?**
**A:** No. The system is private - only PTA officers can manage the system.
