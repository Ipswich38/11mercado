# 📱 11Mercado PTA SMS Registration Guide
## Attendance Notification System for Parents

**Welcome to 11Mercado PTA's innovative SMS attendance system!** Get instant notifications when your child arrives at or leaves school.

---

## 🚀 **How to Register (Parents)**

### **Step 1: Send Registration SMS**
From your Globe mobile phone, send this text message with your child's **LASTNAME**:

**Text Message:** `JOIN [YOUR CHILD'S LASTNAME]`  
**Send to:** `21666946` (Globe/TM users) or `225646946` (other networks)

**Examples:**
- If your child is "DELA CRUZ, MARIA", text: `JOIN DELA CRUZ`
- If your child is "SANTOS, JUAN", text: `JOIN SANTOS`
- If your child is "REYES, ANNA", text: `JOIN REYES`

### **Step 2: Confirm Subscription**
You'll receive a confirmation message from Globe Labs asking you to confirm. Simply reply `YES` to activate.

### **Step 3: Welcome Message**
Once confirmed, you'll receive a welcome message from 11Mercado PTA confirming your registration.

---

## 📨 **What SMS Notifications You'll Receive**

### **Present Notification:**
> *11Mercado PTA Alert: [Student Name] marked PRESENT today (Sep 7, 2025 at 7:30 AM). Safe learning environment confirmed. ✅*

### **Absent Notification:**
> *11Mercado PTA Alert: [Student Name] marked ABSENT today (Sep 7, 2025 at 8:00 AM). Reason: Sick with fever. Please ensure student safety. ⚠️*

### **Late Notification:**
> *11Mercado PTA Alert: [Student Name] marked LATE today (Sep 7, 2025 at 8:15 AM). Student has arrived safely. 🕐*

## ❓ **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: Is this service free?**
**A:** Yes! The SMS notifications are sent through your existing Globe unlimited text plan at no extra cost.

### **Q: What if I'm not a Globe subscriber?**
**A:** You can still register using the cross-telco short code `225646946`, but standard SMS rates may apply.

### **Q: How do I unsubscribe?**
**A:** Simply text `STOP` to the same number you registered with.

### **Q: Can I register multiple children?**
**A:** Yes! Just inform the PTA officer to link your phone number to all your children's records.

### **Q: What if I don't receive notifications?**
**A:** Contact the Grade 11 Mercado section president or PTA office for assistance.

### **Q: Is my phone number secure?**
**A:** Absolutely! Your phone number is only used for attendance notifications and is stored securely.

## 🔧 **For PTA Officers: Linking Parents to Students**

### **Step 1: Access Parent Phone Manager**
1. Open 11Mercado PTA app
2. Go to **Attendance Tracker**
3. Click **"Manage Parent Phones"**

### **Step 2: Link Registered Parents**
1. **Left Panel:** Shows all students
2. **Right Panel:** Shows parents who have registered via SMS
3. **Link Process:** Select a student, then click "Link" next to the parent's phone number

### **Step 3: Manual Phone Entry (If Needed)**
- Click on a student name
- Enter the parent's phone number manually
- Click the green checkmark to save
